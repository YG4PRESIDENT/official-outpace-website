More help ↗
When can I get Outpace?+

The new Outpace app is preparing for release on iPhone and Android. Download links will appear here once it’s available.

What does it cost?+

The first release is free. Optional play points have no cash value and cannot be bought, withdrawn, donated or redeemed. This release has no paid entries or cash prizes.

When does the daily round end?+

Friends finish at 8 p.m. Pacific. Groups use their selected timezone, with an 8 p.m.–8 p.m. round. Open the app and sync before the finish; later uploads don’t change locked results.

Do I need a smartwatch?+

No smartwatch is required. Outpace reads available step totals through Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. Phone or watch records need to reach your health app first.

Who sees my steps and photos?+

Competition totals are visible to your mutual friends and current group members. Winner photos are reviewed before sharing and delivered only to authorized viewers. Your calendar history is private to your account.
