A hand with Outpace.

Send us a private request

In Outpace, open your profile avatar → Settings → Help & feedback. Choose Get help, An idea or Safety. Your message goes to our support queue and our reply appears on that page.

For account access or privacy requests, email support@virtualpiratesllc.com. Please leave out passwords, verification codes and medical details.

Steps haven’t updated?

Open Outpace and refresh before the 8 p.m. finish. Phone and watch records must first reach Apple Health or Health Connect. Outpace syncs when opened or resumed, while active and on manual refresh. It does not currently promise background sync.

Your photo isn’t showing?

New photos are saved privately and reviewed before sharing. Check Winner photo in Settings for its review status. You can replace a rejected image there.

Remove your account

Open your avatar → Settings → Account & privacy → Delete account. The app explains what happens to your groups, history and play points before you confirm.
