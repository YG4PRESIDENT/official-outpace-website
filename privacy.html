Outpace Privacy

Updated October 8, 2026. Operated by Vrtl Pirates LLC. Outpace is a friends and groups step competition app for people 18 and older.

Account and competition data

Clerk verifies your chosen sign-in method and stores its account and verified contact information. Social sign-in may supply a suggested name and an email or Apple relay address. Convex stores your profile name, social connections, group memberships, competition step totals and sync times, private calendar activity history, optional daily walking distance and active calories, activity preferences, profile icon, hidden-friend preferences, winner photos, reactions, reports, city-area preference and non-redeemable play-point ledger. Authentication and hosting providers also process network/device and security metadata needed to operate their services.

Health access

Step access is optional. Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android supply step totals for the competition windows you join. Onboarding requests steps only. If you enable walking distance or active calories in Activity settings, Outpace separately requests access and stores those daily aggregates privately. They do not affect rankings. Outpace does not write health records or upload individual samples, workout routes or precise location. Totals refresh when you open/resume the app and while it is active. Your current group members and mutually accepted friends can see the intended shared competition totals. Revoke access in your device settings.

Photos and social visibility

Photos are reviewed by Outpace before publication to friends or groups. Uploaded photos are decoded and re-encoded to remove image location metadata. Shared winner images are delivered only after checking current access. Group admins can remove reported images; a friends-circle viewer can remove an image from their own circle. Blocking hides reactions and photos across that connection. Shared-group competitive records remain governed by group membership.

City-area discovery

You may choose a broad US city area manually. Outpace does not request GPS for this feature. Only public groups whose admin explicitly opted in appear. No nearby-person directory is offered. Clear your area, hide groups or turn off a group’s discovery preference inside the app.

Play points

Points have no cash value and cannot be bought, withdrawn, donated or redeemed. A points group requires the same disclosed daily entry amount from every competitor, with explicit automatic-entry consent. There are no real deposits, payouts or charitable transfers.

Retention and deletion

Unused earlier image assets are eligible for cleanup after 90 days. Active default photos and photos referenced by retained results remain until account or relevant group deletion. You can request account deletion inside Outpace: access closes immediately and a retryable server workflow removes personal step history, photos, social data and Clerk identity. Shared groups transfer administration; sole-member groups are deleted. Historical results retain anonymous places without your name, health totals or photo. Future held entries are canceled; already-started entries remain anonymous escrow until the round settles. Unused noncash points disappear. A minimal deletion tombstone prevents old sessions from restoring a deleted account. Provider backup retention follows the providers’ configured policies.

Support and diagnostics

Help & feedback stores your message privately for Outpace support; replies appear in the app. Please do not include passwords, verification codes or medical details. Redacted app diagnostics contain a fixed error code, platform, build and timestamp, expire after 30 days and are removed with account deletion. Image review records and personal support requests are removed with account deletion.

Controls and support

You can change your profile/default image, manage friends and blocks, leave groups, revoke health access, clear city preferences, report content and delete your account. To ask for help or exercise data rights, contact support@virtualpiratesllc.com.

Changes

The version displayed inside the app and this document identify the current data practices. Material changes will be disclosed before introducing new collection or a money feature.
