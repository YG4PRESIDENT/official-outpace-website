Play fair. Keep moving.

Outpace is operated by Vrtl Pirates LLC for adults 18 and older. This release offers free social competition and non-redeemable play points. There are no paid entries, cash prizes or charitable transfers.

Outpace’s free and play-point competitions are sponsored and administered by Vrtl Pirates LLC. Apple is not a sponsor and is not involved in these competitions in any manner.

The daily finish

Groups use one chosen timezone and an 8 p.m.–8 p.m. round. Friends use the Pacific clock. Eligibility starts with your first full round after joining and activation. Sync by 8 p.m.; results lock then. Later syncs can update activity history without changing locked awards. A confirmed zero is eligible; unavailable data is unranked.

Photos and badges

Tied leaders share first rank. A stored server draw selects one tied leader to choose the header photo. Their approved default is used if they do not choose another. Photos require review before sharing. Weekly and monthly tied leaders all receive badges; weeks run Monday–Sunday and months follow the calendar by each round’s closing date.

Play points

Verified accounts receive 1,000 points once. Points cannot be bought, redeemed, withdrawn or donated. A points group has one fixed daily stake for everyone, disclosed before automatic-entry consent. The first full entry is reserved immediately; future entries are prepared before the next round starts. Insufficient points mean sitting out that round’s prize while remaining a member. Missed reservations are never charged retroactively.

Tied prizes split deterministically, separately from the image draw. Leaving before a reserved round starts returns its points. Leaving or deleting an account after the start forfeits that entry into the pool. Group cancellation or a round with no available step data refunds surviving accounts’ held entries. Unused points disappear on account deletion.

Your people

Public groups allow anyone to join. Private groups require an invite. Nearby discovery shows only opted-in public groups in a broad city area selected manually. It does not use GPS or list nearby people. Hiding a friend changes only your roster; blocking hides that connection’s photos and reactions. Shared-group competition records follow group membership.

Respect the group

Do not post harassment, sexual content, graphic violence, spam or private information about someone else. Only upload photos you have permission to share. Report content in the app and block unwanted connections. Group admins can remove winner images; Outpace can reject photos and remove groups from discovery following review.

Help and account deletion

Open your avatar → Settings → Help & feedback to contact Outpace. Replies appear there. Delete your account from Account & privacy. Access closes immediately while the server completes removal. Shared groups transfer administration and past results retain anonymous places without your personal totals, name or photos.
