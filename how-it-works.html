THE OUTPACE WAY

Your usual day.
A better reason
to move.

Outpace turns everyday steps into friendly competition. Here’s what happens from your first invite to the evening finish.

01

Make yourself at home.

Sign in, choose the name your friends know and save a default winner photo. Confirm you’re 18 or older, read the rules and choose whether to connect steps now or later.

Outpace reads steps from Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android. Connecting health is optional. Without an available step count, you stay unranked.

02

Find your people.

Friends is your starting place. Invite people you know and compare steps once you’ve both connected. Groups give a crew its own persistent leaderboard, with a chosen timezone.

Public groups can be joined; private groups require an invite. Nearby discovery shows opted-in public groups in a broad city area you select yourself. It doesn’t use GPS or show nearby people.

03

Keep the board moving.

Steps refresh when you open or resume Outpace, while it’s active and when you refresh manually. Make sure your phone or watch has synced with your health app, then open Outpace before the finish.

React to the leaderboard and encourage your friends. Background step delivery isn’t promised, so don’t rely on an unopened app to submit your final total.

04

Eight o’clock. Your moment.

Friends rounds close at 8 p.m. Pacific. Each group closes at 8 p.m. in its chosen timezone. Your first full round is your first eligible round.

The daily winner’s approved photo fills the header until the next winner takes over. Tied leaders share first place; a stored draw chooses one person’s photo. All tied weekly and monthly leaders earn badges.

The complete rules ↗
05

Your progress belongs to you.

Explore your private activity calendar and choose a personal step goal. Optional walking distance and active calories can be enabled separately in Activity settings; they don’t affect rankings.

Manage photos, friends, blocks and permissions. Support and account deletion are available inside the app, with help here if you can’t sign in.

Get a hand ↗
Friendly competition. Free first release.

Outpace is for adults 18 and older. Optional play points have no cash value. This release has no paid entries, cash prizes, deposits or withdrawals.
